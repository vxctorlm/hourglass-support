The essentials

Hourglass requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking or analytics. Focus data and civilization progress are stored locally and are not sent to the developer.

1. Who is responsible

Hourglass is provided by Víctor López Moreno, Spain (the “developer”, “Hourglass”, “we”, or “us”). For privacy questions, rights requests or deletion requests, email vxctor@automore.es.

2. Scope and information used

This policy applies to Hourglass for macOS, iOS and iPadOS, its macOS web-filter extension, and this privacy page.

Information stored on your device

Hourglass stores the selected language; focus durations, break settings and strictness; selected apps and domains on macOS; an active-session snapshot; up to 90 completed-session history entries containing completion date, completed rounds and focused minutes; and the state and history of the fictional civilization. This information is stored with Apple system storage on the device.

System backups are controlled by your Apple and device settings. Hourglass does not operate a cloud synchronization service, and civilization progress is not synchronized between devices.

macOS application blocking

If you enable application blocking, Hourglass stores the bundle identifiers and names of the apps you select. During an active focus round it observes which app becomes active, compares its bundle identifier locally with your list, and closes it when there is a match. The current app name and icon may be shown briefly in a local reminder. Hourglass does not create or transmit a history of the apps you open.

macOS website filtering

If you authorize website protection, the Hourglass system extension receives network-flow destination information supplied by macOS and compares the destination hostname locally with the domains you selected. Matching connections are blocked. The extension does not inspect page content, record browsing history or transmit visited hostnames to us. The selected domain list, filter status and a rules revision number are stored locally in the Hourglass App Group so the app and extension can share the rules.

iPhone and iPad

The iOS and iPadOS versions do not inspect, close or block other apps and do not filter websites. They contain no Network Extension and no external updater.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any files or diagnostic details you choose to include. Please do not send information that is not needed to resolve your request.

3. Updates and external connections

The Developer ID version for macOS uses the open-source Sparkle framework to check an Hourglass appcast hosted on GitHub Pages and, when you approve an update, to download it from the location listed in that feed. These HTTPS requests necessarily disclose standard connection data to the hosting provider, such as the IP address, date and time, request headers, and app or user-agent version. Hourglass does not enable Sparkle system profiling and does not add hardware, language or other custom profile fields to update requests.

GitHub may retain standard service and security logs under its own policy. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data-collection notice.

Versions distributed through Apple are delivered through Apple services. Apple may independently process App Store, download, purchase, device and diagnostic information under the Apple Privacy Policy. Hourglass does not receive your Apple ID, payment-card or complete transaction information.

4. What we do not collect

Hourglass has no developer-operated backend and does not collect or transmit account details, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, health data, advertising identifiers, browsing history, focus history or civilization data. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, social login, in-app purchase, artificial-intelligence service or cross-app tracking.

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use personal information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Decisions made inside the civilization are fictional gameplay generated locally.

5. Purposes and legal bases

  • App functionality: keep your chosen settings, restore timers, maintain local history and civilization progress, and provide the macOS blocking features you request. Processing occurs on your device to provide the requested app functionality.
  • Updates, security and reliability: retrieve signed update information and downloads for the Developer ID macOS version, based on providing the service and our legitimate interest in keeping Hourglass secure and current.
  • Support: answer messages and troubleshoot issues, based on taking steps at your request and our legitimate interest in supporting the app.
  • Legal compliance: meet applicable legal, security and consumer-protection obligations.

6. Sharing and international processing

We do not receive or share the locally stored app data described above. Limited network information may be processed independently by Apple when distributing the app or providing operating-system services, and by GitHub when serving the macOS update feed or downloads. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Apple and GitHub may process information outside your country using the safeguards described in their policies.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local preferences, focus history and civilization progress remain until you reset them, remove the relevant data through the app where available, or uninstall Hourglass. Completed-session history is limited to the latest 90 entries.
  • An active-session snapshot is removed when the session is stopped or completed.
  • macOS filter rules remain locally until changed, cleared, or Hourglass and its system extension are removed. You can disable the extension in macOS System Settings.
  • Server logs generated by Apple or GitHub are retained under their respective policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain necessary security records and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized.

You may request deletion of information held directly by us at vxctor@automore.es. We may ask for reasonable verification. We cannot remotely access or erase data stored only on your device, or records controlled solely by Apple or GitHub.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing; receive portable data; withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing; and lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. We will respond to valid requests without undue delay and generally within one month, subject to any lawful extension. In Spain, you may contact the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

You can change local settings in Hourglass, stop a session, clear or reset available app data, disable or remove the macOS filter in System Settings, control backups in Apple settings, and uninstall the app to remove its local container subject to operating-system backup behavior.

9. Children

Hourglass is a general-audience productivity app and is not specifically directed to children below the minimum digital-consent age in their country. The app does not request a child’s name, email address, location or contact details. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information through a support request may contact us for review and deletion.

10. Security, this website and changes

Hourglass uses Apple platform protections, local data minimization, a sandboxed App Group for macOS filter rules, and signed update verification. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

This static privacy page contains no first-party cookies, advertising, analytics or contact forms. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its own notice. We may update this policy when Hourglass, its providers or applicable law changes. Material changes will be reflected by the date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

Las copias de seguridad del sistema dependen de tus ajustes de Apple y del dispositivo. Hourglass no opera un servicio de sincronización en la nube y el progreso de la civilización no se sincroniza entre dispositivos.

Bloqueo de aplicaciones en macOS

Si activas el bloqueo de aplicaciones, Hourglass guarda los identificadores y nombres de las apps que eliges. Durante una ronda de concentración observa qué app pasa a primer plano, compara localmente su identificador con tu lista y la cierra si coincide. El nombre e icono de esa app pueden mostrarse brevemente en un recordatorio local. Hourglass no crea ni transmite un historial de las aplicaciones que abres.

Filtrado de sitios web en macOS

Si autorizas la protección web, la extensión de sistema de Hourglass recibe la información de destino de los flujos de red que facilita macOS y compara localmente el nombre de host con los dominios seleccionados. Las conexiones coincidentes se bloquean. La extensión no inspecciona el contenido de las páginas, no registra el historial de navegación ni transmite a nosotros los hosts visitados. La lista de dominios, el estado del filtro y un número de revisión de reglas se guardan localmente en el App Group de Hourglass para que la app y la extensión compartan las reglas.

iPhone y iPad

Las versiones para iOS y iPadOS no inspeccionan, cierran ni bloquean otras apps y no filtran sitios web. No incorporan Network Extension ni un actualizador externo.

Soporte

Si nos escribes por correo, recibimos tu dirección de email, el mensaje y los archivos o datos de diagnóstico que decidas incluir. No envíes información innecesaria para resolver la consulta.

保存在设备上的信息

Hourglass 会保存所选语言；专注时长、休息设置和严格程度；macOS 上选择的应用与域名；当前会话快照；最多 90 条已完成会话记录（包括完成日期、完成轮次和专注分钟数）；以及虚构文明的状态与历史。这些信息通过 Apple 的系统存储保存在设备上。

macOS 应用阻止

macOS 网站过滤

如果授权网站保护，Hourglass 系统扩展会接收 macOS 提供的网络流目标信息，并在本地将目标主机名与你选择的域名比较。匹配的连接会被阻止。扩展不会检查网页内容、记录浏览历史或向我们传输访问过的主机名。所选域名、过滤状态和规则修订号会保存在 Hourglass 的本地 App Group 中，供应用与扩展共享规则。

iPhone 与 iPad
