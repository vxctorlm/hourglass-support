Privacy, made clear.

The essentials

Hourglass requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking or analytics. Focus data and civilization progress are stored locally and are not sent to the developer.

1. Who is responsible

Hourglass is provided by Víctor López Moreno, Spain (the “developer”, “Hourglass”, “we”, or “us”). For privacy questions, rights requests or deletion requests, email vxctor@automore.es.

2. Scope and information used

This policy applies to Hourglass for macOS, iOS and iPadOS, its macOS web-filter extension, and this privacy page.

Information stored on your device

Hourglass stores the selected language; focus durations, break settings and strictness; selected apps and domains on macOS; an active-session snapshot; up to 90 completed-session history entries containing completion date, completed rounds and focused minutes; and the state and history of the fictional civilization. This information is stored with Apple system storage on the device.

System backups are controlled by your Apple and device settings. Hourglass does not operate a cloud synchronization service, and civilization progress is not synchronized between devices.

macOS application blocking

If you enable application blocking, Hourglass stores the bundle identifiers and names of the apps you select. During an active focus round it observes which app becomes active, compares its bundle identifier locally with your list, and closes it when there is a match. The current app name and icon may be shown briefly in a local reminder. Hourglass does not create or transmit a history of the apps you open.

macOS website filtering

If you authorize website protection, the Hourglass system extension receives network-flow destination information supplied by macOS and compares the destination hostname locally with the domains you selected. Matching connections are blocked. The extension does not inspect page content, record browsing history or transmit visited hostnames to us. The selected domain list, filter status and a rules revision number are stored locally in the Hourglass App Group so the app and extension can share the rules.

iPhone and iPad

The iOS and iPadOS versions do not inspect, close or block other apps and do not filter websites. They contain no Network Extension and no external updater.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any files or diagnostic details you choose to include. Please do not send information that is not needed to resolve your request.
